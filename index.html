Spell Listener

Not listening

Say a spell, see its rules

Press Start listening, then say something like “I cast Fireball.” Add a level to see what to roll: “I cast Burning Hands at level 2.” Spells that are also everyday words, like Light or Shield, need a casting word: “I cast Shield.”

Combat rules work too: say “I jump” or “I dash.” Common words need “action”: “I take the Help action.”
